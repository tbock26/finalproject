Welcome to Open Studios

Where learning and fun collide.

Open Studios provides educational resources for kids K-12 to learn more about art practice and art history. In an age of quickly accessible information, we aim to provide an engaging platform for students to learn deeply, to spend quality time with art, and to bring their skills out into the world. On this site, students are able to discover new elements of art history, develop their own creations, and test themselves to solidify their knowledge as they go.
