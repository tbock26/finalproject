About the Authors

D.K. Rule is a freelance writer and Library Science graduate student in Brooklyn, NY. She focuses on mental health, the power of art making, and cultural commentary in her work. She has been published in Poetose Journal and HOW blog, in addition to others. DK also plays the guitar, knits and crochets, and paints in oil and watercolor. Check out dkrule.com for more. For this project, D.K. created and organized the Home Page (index.html) and CSS files, as well as built the Paint Page.

Annie Dauber is an arts worker, writer and student, currently based in Brooklyn. She attends the Pratt Institute and is in her first year of the MLIS program. She worked as a gallery registrar in San Francisco before moving to New York, and is currently the Exhibitions and Collections Management and Photography Curatorial Intern at the Morgan Library & Museum. She is also a freelance writer, and has published critical work in Juxtapoz Magazine, Sixty Inches From Center, and Woven Tale Press. For this project, Annie created the logo, wrote content for the home page, and did coding and content for the quiz, parent FAQ, and at-home activities.
